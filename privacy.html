Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This website belongs to Auto Repair Emmanuel LLC, 2834 N Graham St, Charlotte, NC 28206, phone (980) 531-2447. This page explains what we do with the information you send us.

What we collect

When you send the booking form, we receive the service you need, your car's make, model and year, your preferred day, your name, phone number, optional email and your description of the problem. We do not ask for payment details on this website.

Why we use it

Only to contact you about your request: to call or text you, confirm a time and prepare for your visit. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Requests are stored in Google Firebase, a service of Google LLC, and can only be read by our staff. If email notifications are turned on, a copy of the request is sent to our business email through EmailJS.

How long we keep it

We keep requests for up to 24 months so we can see your repair history, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time.

Third-party content

The map on our home page is provided by Google Maps, and the fonts by Google Fonts. Google may collect technical data such as your IP address when they load. See Google's privacy policy. This site does not use advertising cookies.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information you sent us, call (980) 531-2447 or ask us at the shop.
