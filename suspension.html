Warning signs

Signs of worn suspension or steering

  • Clunking or knocking over bumps
  • The car bounces or dips when braking
  • The car drifts or pulls to one side
  • Steering feels loose or wanders
  • Uneven tire wear
  • Humming or vibration at speed

FAQ

Suspension questions

How do I know my shocks or struts are worn?

The car bounces after bumps, dips when you brake or leans in turns. Leaking fluid on a shock is another sign.

Is a clunking noise dangerous?

Often it's a worn link or bushing, but it can be a ball joint, and a failed ball joint is dangerous. Have it checked soon.

Will my car need an alignment after the repair?

Often yes, after replacing tie rods or struts. We'll tell you if yours needs one.
