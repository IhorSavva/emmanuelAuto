Warning signs

When to get your brakes checked

  • Squeaking or squealing when you brake
  • Grinding, metal-on-metal noise
  • The car pulls to one side when braking
  • The pedal feels soft or sinks to the floor
  • The steering wheel shakes when you brake
  • The brake warning light is on

FAQ

Brake questions

How often do brake pads need replacing?

It depends on how and where you drive — often every 30,000 to 70,000 miles. We measure your pads and tell you how much is left.

Can I keep driving if my brakes grind?

Grinding usually means the pads are worn to the metal and are damaging the rotors. Drive as little as possible and call us.

Do I always need new rotors with new pads?

No. If your rotors are within spec and not warped, pads may be enough. We show you what we measured.
