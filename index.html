Services

What we fix

From a check-engine light to a worn-out axle. Not sure what's wrong? Start with a diagnostic and we'll find the cause.

Diagnostics & check engine

Computer diagnostics for warning lights, misfires, strange noises and leaks. We find the cause before we replace any parts.

Brakes & rotors

Pads, rotors, calipers and brake fluid service. Squeaking, grinding or a soft pedal? Don't wait on brakes.

Suspension & steering

Sway bar links, shocks, struts, ball joints and tie rods — for a car that drives straight and quiet again.

Electrical & batteries

Alternators, starters, batteries and wiring. If your car won't start or the lights flicker, we'll track it down.

Engine repair

Misfires, ignition coils, spark plugs, oil leaks and overheating — repaired right the first time.

Axles & drivetrain

CV axles, rear axle assemblies, wheel bearings and driveline noises, plus oil changes and routine maintenance.
